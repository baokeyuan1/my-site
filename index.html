About Me

I am a first-year undergraduate at Westlake University in Hangzhou. Although I have not declared a major yet, my direction is fundamental research in artificial intelligence. I was introduced to competitive programming in middle school and never really stopped: this October I represented the university at the 2026 ICPC Asia Xi'an Regional Contest, where our team took a bronze medal.

Right now I am doing two things in parallel — keeping up with contest training, and building the mathematical and algorithmic foundations for AI. Farther out, I would like to work on algorithm optimization: making models and systems faster and cheaper without giving up what they can do.
